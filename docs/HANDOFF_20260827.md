## WHAT IS SETTLED

### The label pipeline works and is validated
- **Phase 0a:** `soiling_srr` fits PVDAQ's daily residential feed. 12/12, then 66/75.
- **Phase 2 PASSES:** 24 systems within 5 km of an NREL station. PVDAQ p50 2.54 vs NREL 2.70,
  paired diff **+0.19, 95% CI [-0.66, +0.99]**, Wilcoxon p 0.46. No detectable bias.
  Caveat that must always travel with it: 881 of NREL's 891 rows came from this same class of
  method, so this is a **reproduction check**, not independent validation.
- **Method noise: SD 0.83 pts** (n=20, same roof/years, 2x2 over irradiance source x module
  temperature coefficient, pooled over both diagonals). **Supersedes the 0.72**, which was
  n=19 with gamma held fixed; the identical statistic at n=20 is 0.78.
- **Within-cell spread 1.46 pts** (resolved-gamma refit, 66 of 75 systems pass QC), 65% of
  variance within cells, so **signal-to-noise 1.76x** (supersedes 1.95x). Enough to fit a
  feature model at n≈988; **not** enough to rank two neighbouring roofs.

### The product does not clear, and this is now measured per-roof
`aoi_cleaning_threshold.py` on all 2,494 geometry-scored arrays: **zero clear.** Best case
loses ~$30. Three causes, in order:
1. **NEC 690.12(B)(2) (eff. 2019-01-01)** effectively mandates MLPE on CA residential roofs,
   and MLPE defuses the substring nonlinearity that made moss expensive (21% string vs 8% MLPE).
2. **`MIN_PRO_SERVICE = $150` binds at every residential size**; median AOI array is 3.5 kW.
3. Only **36 of 3,362** arrays match a permit with both pre-2017 hardware and NEM 2.0, and
   they are smaller than average. Zero of them clear even assuming string inverters.

### Three channels, not two
| | dust | mineral band | biological |
|---|---|---|---|
| recovery_frac | 0.045 measured | 0.36 modelled | 0.39-0.61 modelled |
| verdict | dead | **dead in Santa Cruz** | live but does not clear |

Mineral band is dead here on two independent lines: AOI build:clear rain ratio 2.00 is below
every documented band site (2.24-4.80), and modelled on real precipitation the band never
accumulates (f = 0.007) because 25.5 heavy-rain days a year flush it.

### Literature position
- **Supports the physics:** Gostein 2015 measured 9% loss from a band covering 0.5% of module
  area; our independent pvlib model gives 9.10%. Zhao et al. 2021 measured 4.7-26.7%.
- **Supports the climate:** Santa Cruz growth-weighted Time of Wetness 2,444 h/yr vs Berkeley
  2,468 (ratio 0.99), and Berkeley is where biofilm on panels was confirmed (Porcar et al. 2018).
- **The gap nobody has filled:** no measurement of biological soiling's POWER COST in a
  Mediterranean climate. Porcar characterised organisms but measured no power loss.
- **Where it does NOT transfer:** the band papers are mineral dust in China at 3-4° tilt;
  Cano's tilt curve is Arizona desert dust; Shirakawa's Brazilian 11% is a diffuse whole-panel
  film, a different loss mechanism from an edge band, and **cannot calibrate our `f`**.

## THE OPEN PROBLEM — **CLOSED 2026-08-27**

**`GAMMA_PDC` had more leverage on a label than the signal we were trying to measure.**
Measured on system 10109: -0.0035 gave 2.841 pts, -0.0045 gave 1.796, a delta of 1.045
against a method-noise SD of 0.72 and a within-cell spread of 1.41. Two scripts disagreed and
a comment asserted they matched.

**It was resolved, not re-documented.** `src/risk/module_gamma.py` looks the coefficient up
per system from PVDAQ module metadata against pvlib's CEC database. Full record in
[`GAMMA_RESOLUTION_20260827.md`](GAMMA_RESOLUTION_20260827.md); the short version:

| tier | share of 1,629 residential systems |
|---|---|
| matched to a named module | **43.3%** |
| matched to a manufacturer | 35.2% |
| matched to a cell technology | 2.0% |
| labelled fleet default | **19.6%** |

- **The recorded labels are biased, not invalidated.** Resolved gamma has median -0.00430,
  SD 0.00045; median |delta| from the -0.0045 they used is 0.00020, and only 9.0% of systems
  are 0.0010 or further out. -0.0045 is the CEC median for both x-Si classes. **-0.0035 was
  the wrong number.**
- **Gamma is real but secondary**: 26.4% of the two contrasts' variance, against the
  irradiance source's 73.6%.
- **Signal-to-noise falls to 1.76x** (1.46 / 0.83) from the quoted 1.95x, and **stays above
  1.0**. Phase 3 can still fit a feature model across ~988 systems; it still cannot rank two
  neighbouring roofs.

Two estimator bugs surfaced on the way, both of which changed the number: the old 0.72 was
n=19 with gamma held fixed (the same statistic is 0.78 at n=20), and a single corner-to-corner
contrast of a 2x2 is not a total-noise estimator, since the two diagonals measure 0.72 and
0.92. `pvdaq_method_noise.py` now pools both.

**Closed on this thread too:** the 75-system resolved-gamma refit is done and fed through.
Within-cell spread moves 1.41 -> **1.46 pts**, which is the predicted small move. Fixing that
run also surfaced a live `NameError` in `pvdaq_within_cluster.py`'s verdict block: a variable
deleted from the rest of the function was still referenced there, so every run printed the
full table and then died — which reads as a clean run unless you check the exit code.

## TRAPS — things that will waste your time

1. **`conda run ... python - <<'PY'` silently does nothing.** Heredocs to stdin fail under
   `conda run`. Write the script to a file and run it.
2. **`conda run` buffers all output until exit**, so a long job's log is empty until it
   finishes. Use file-existence polling, not log tailing.
3. **Background jobs die at session end** unless launched with `setsid nohup ... </dev/null &`
   plus `disown`. Two runs were lost this way.
4. **The scratchpad is cleared between sessions.** Anything referenced later goes in `outputs/`.
5. **PVGIS serves 2005-2023 only.** Outside that it is a hard HTTPError; clamp the years.
6. **Open-Meteo free tier 429s** under load. Irradiance is cached per 0.5° cell over a fixed
   era window; keep it that way.
7. **`pvdaq/parquet/pvdata/` is a partial mirror** (157 systems, zero Csb). Use
   `pvdaq/csv/pvdata/system_id=<id>/`.
8. **Do not quote:** the fleet degradation correlation (rejected, implausible magnitudes and
   confounded), the n=3 method-noise figure of 0.12 (luck; the real one is 0.72), or the
   `perfect_clean`/`half_norm_clean` gap of ~7 pts (one-sided by construction).
9. **`np.interp` clamps, it does not extrapolate.** This caused two bugs already.
10. **Every bug found so far was silent and optimistic.** Nothing threw; tests passed. Assume
    the next one looks like a plausible number, not a crash.
