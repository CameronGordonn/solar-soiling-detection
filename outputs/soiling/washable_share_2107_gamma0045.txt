  gap (perfect - half_norm)  4.826 pts of annual output
  perfect_clean forces every soiling interval to start at SR = 1.0, which is
  the branch NREL's published map takes. half_norm_clean fits the start of
  each interval below 1.0 instead, so the gap is the standing deficit that
  the resets in this record do not clear. It is a LEVEL, not a rate: it does
  not divide by the length of the record. Whether it accumulates is the
  separate question CODS answers below.
  Note what the resets here mostly ARE: rain. The method labels recovery
  events, not their cause, and no wash is recorded in this dataset. So this
  number is the layer RAIN leaves behind. How much of it a paid wash removes
  is not in this data and this script does not claim it.

  performance at the START of each of the 40 valid intervals, i.e. just
  after a reset. 1.0 is a full recovery; the gap above is what these fall short by:

  These inherit the weakness of the input: 64% daily coverage means many
  resets are only partly observed, and a reset seen late reads as incomplete.
